I asked AI to teach me something. Every recommendation was that of a 'paid' version of the software. ( over a week )

Coincidence ? Sure ! lets give benefit of doubt.

Then I explicitly asked AI to use an opensource LLM. Again the recommendation I got was that of the 'paid' version of the software.

The third time, I asked the question to use a locally running LLM. This time I received a partial answer for what I wanted.

Bias - preference to give an information.
Friction - resistance to give information.

Moral of the story ...

✓ Biases and friction, in LLMs are big revenue pipelines.

✓ Advertisers can and WILL buy biases in LLMs. Maybe they already are.

✓ Some orgs are planning to fully automate their systems via AI. They will be the sheep to fleece in the future. Injecting a minor bias or friction will raise the bill whenever needed.

✓ Worst part - finding such biases would be difficult. I think really difficult.
