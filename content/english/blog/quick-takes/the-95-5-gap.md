AI is **probabilistic**. 95% remarkable, 5% broken. Software engineering is **deterministic**. 100% or nothing. That gap is reshaping who stays and who thrives. Here's my unwarranted opinion.

**For engineers:** You need both skills now. Get very good at extracting that 95% from AI. That's survival—anyone without this is already replaceable. But here's where you actually build a career: solve the remaining 5% with precision. You're the person who knows when AI is hallucinating. Who catches the edge case. Who writes the deterministic code that doesn't fail in production. That's where your value lives.

**For leaders:** If your team can't work with the 95%, they're expendable. If they work with the 95% but can't solve the 5%, they're mid-tier. The ones fluent in both? Who know when to lean on AI speed and when to lean on engineering rigor? They're in demand everywhere. The gap between 95% and 100% isn't small. It's where careers get built.
