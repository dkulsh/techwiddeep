🚨 FREE ki Advice for Job Seekers

If you're showing up for an interview, here's how NOT to self-destroy:

🔹 **Be on Time** – Not 5 minutes late. Not "just joining." Be there on time. It shows respect.

🔹 **Look the Part** – Shave. Comb your hair. Wear a decent shirt. And for the love of clean code, IRON IT. An unshaved face, crumpled shirt etc. create poor impressions. These tell others that we don't take minor things seriously. Whether in software or in life. First impressions matter – even virtually.

🔹 **Check Yourself (Literally)** – Is your camera angled like a thriller movie? Is your face half-lit like a horror scene? Mic working? WiFi stable? Fix these before the interview starts.

🔹 **Know What's on Your Resume** – Yes, buzzwords can get you noticed, but please, understand what you've written. If an interviewer asks you about it, you can't play hide and seek.

And remember 👇

Common Sense ! some things money can't buy. For everything else, there's Mastercard. 😉
