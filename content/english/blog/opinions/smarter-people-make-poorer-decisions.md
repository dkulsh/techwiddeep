Think being smart makes you immune to bad calls? Think again! It’s tempting to believe that brainpower shields us from fake news or shaky reasoning, but here’s the kicker: **the sharper your mind, the trickier your biases can be.** Let’s unravel this mind-bending paradox with a dash of humor and a whole lot of truth. Ready to question your smarts?

#### The Myth of the Bulletproof Brain


The popular wisdom is that the smarter you are, the less susceptible you are to fake news. After all, part of being "smart" is being good at processing information.

So, it feels like smart people should have the ability to spot flawed reasoning and fight it. After all, **being “smart” means you’re a pro at processing data, right?**

Wrong! **surprisingly, being smart can actually make our biases worse.**

A different frame:  {{< bigtext >}}The smarter you are, the better you are at spinning a story to fit your beliefs.{{< /bigtext >}}


After all, people in the political "spin room" are generally pretty smart for a reason. **Smartness doesn’t save you; it can make you a better storyteller for your own biases.**


##### The Bias Blind Spot

In 2012, psychologists tested the blind-spot bias, meaning people who are better at recognizing biased reasoning in others but are blind to bias in themselves.

**In six of the seven biases tested, "more *intelligent* participants showed larger bias blind spots."**

Dan Kahan found: "The better you are with numbers, the better you are at spinning those numbers to conform to and support your beliefs."

This insight leads us to explore why we can't always overcome these tendencies.

## Can't Help It


Unfortunately, this is just the way evolution built us. **We are wired to protect our beliefs even when our goal is to truth-seek.**

**This is one of those instances where being smart doesn't help us**. As with visual illusions, we can't make our minds work differently than they do, no matter how smart we are.


So, what’s the takeaway? Being smart doesn’t make you bias-proof—it might even make you more creative at fooling yourself. **The real trick is catching yourself in the act.** Next time you’re dead-set on a belief, pause and ask: **Am I reasoning, or am I rationalizing?** It’s not easy, but it’s the first step to outsmarting your own smarts.
